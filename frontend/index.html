Enforces strict geographical isolation. Any instance residing outside the designated jurisdiction (e.g. India DPDP Act or EU GDPR) is marked infeasible.
Maximum Budget Limit Enforcement HARD CONSTRAINT
Computes total financial cost: (hourlyPrice × duration) + (storagePrice × GB × duration/730). Discards instances exceeding the user-specified maximum budget cap.
